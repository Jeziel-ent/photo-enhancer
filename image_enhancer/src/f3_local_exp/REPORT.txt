1. FORMULA / IMPLEMENTATION
==============================================================================

Baseline F3 (enhance.py:569-580):
    sc  = bandpass(SR_L - Orig_L, sigma=2.5)
    dL  = W * clip(sc, -CLIP, +CLIP) * edge_gate(Orig_L, t0=0.30)
    l_new = L_orig + clip(dL, -ENV, +ENV)          <-- flat ±14 envelope
    LAB[:,0] = clip(l_new, 0, 255)

Tested self-envelope variants (f3_local_exp/recipe.py):

  A) Morphological 3×3 (f3_self_env_tight, ksize=3):
       lo = erode(L_orig, 3×3)
       hi = dilate(L_orig, 3×3)
       l_new = L_orig + clip(dL, lo-L_orig, hi-L_orig)    <-- NO global cap

  B) Morphological 5×5 + global cap (f3_self_env_capped, ksize=5, cap=±10):
       lo = erode(L_orig, 5×5)
       hi = dilate(L_orig, 5×5)
       l_new = L_orig + clip(clip(dL, lo-L_orig, hi-L_orig), -10, +10)

  C) Hybrid 50% (f3_hybrid, ksize=5, blend=0.5):
       l_new = L_orig + 0.5·clip(dL, lo-L_orig, hi-L_orig)
                   + 0.5·clip(dL, -14, +14)

  D) Local-variance envelope (f3_local_contrast_env, k=15, scale=1.0, cap=14):
       local_std = sqrt(Gauss(L²) - Gauss(L)²)
       env = clip(scale · local_std, 0.5, 14)
       l_new = L_orig + clip(dL, -env, +env)

==============================================================================
5. FIDELITY RISKS
==============================================================================

Variant A (k3, no cap) — RISK: SEVERE
  • PSNR regression: −0.3 to −4.6 dB across 7 images (fails harness by up
    to 4.6× tolerance)
  • SSIM regression: −0.004 to −0.014 (fails harness by up to 1.4× tol)
  • Sharpness explosion (130 → 755 on image 5): energy is ringed oscillations
    around edges, not genuine detail. Measurable as "sharpness" but
    perceptually the image looks aliased/artifact-heavy.
  • HALO at all strong edges (building silhouettes, sign frames, window
    frames) — confirmed by PSNR collapse in sign/bldg ROIs.

Variant B (cap10) — RISK: SIGNIFICANT (capability loss)
  • PSNR improved (+1.6 to +2.2 dB) — output is CLOSER to faithful.
  • SSIM improved (+0.004 to +0.014) — output is structurally closer.
  • BUT: sharpness drops 25–35% across ALL images (118→76 on img 1,
    292→224 on img 5, 269→183 on img 2). The engine's primary value
    (perceived sharpness gain from 6–28× to 4–18×) is materially reduced.
  • Mechanism: ±10 global cap < baseline ±14 → F3 contributes less to edges
    → downstream G7-MS/A+ sharpen a softer F3 output → net gain lower.
  • The "self-envelope" is effectively a flat ±10 cap (local range at edge-
    pixels is almost always > 10, see Δeltagate_diag.py). The locally-
    derived component is non-operative; only the lower global cap matters.

Variant C (hybrid_50) — RISK: SEVERE (same as k3)
  • PSNR: −1.2 to −3.6 dB across 7 images.
  • Sharpness explosion (same ringing mechanism as k3).
  • 50/50 blend with the flat cap doesn't prevent edge ringing; the 50%
    self-envelope contribution at edges dominates the high-frequency energy.

Variant D (local-σ, cap=14) — RISK: NONE (but no benefit)
  • PSNR Δ < 0.07, SSIM Δ < 0.001, sharpness Δ < 1%.
  • Near-identical to baseline everywhere.
  • WHY: at edge-gated pixels, local σ (Gaussian kernel radius 15/6 ≈ 2.5)
    exceeds 14 on 99.9–100% of active pixels → envelope saturates at cap 14
    → identical to flat ±14. Only pixels where local σ < 14 AND gate > 0
    (estimated < 2% of image) see any tightening, and those pixels have
    tiny dL already (edge gate ∝ normalized gradient).

==============================================================================
6. ROOT CAUSE: WHY SELF-ENVELOPE FAILS FOR F3
==============================================================================

Diagnostic (deltagate_diag.py) quantifies why:
  • At F3-active pixels (gate > 0.05): 10–17% of all pixels.
  • Raw weighted delta (dL before any envelope): median 12–34, p99 = 108
    (hits inner clip ceiling: W × CLIP = 6 × 18 = 108).
  • Local morphological range (hi − lo) at ksize=5: median 36–106, p90 = 72–189,
    max up to 255.  99.9–100% of active pixels have local range > 14.

KEY INSIGHT:
  F3's delta is concentrated at high-contrast edges (by the edge gate).
  At those exact pixels, the local [min, max] range of the ORIGINAL
  is ALWAYS wider than the flat ±14 cap (99.9–100% of active pixels).
  A morphological self-envelope at those pixels is therefore ALWAYS
  wider than ±14 — the flat cap is the binding constraint.

  In G7-MS/A+/G9, the self-envelope works because their deltas are small
  self-contained high-pass residuals (gain ~1.3–1.5), well within local range.
  F3's delta is a cross-image band-pass scaled by w=6.0 with raw values
  up to 108, which vastly exceeds the local brightness range.

  Result: any morphological self-envelope either:
    (a) WIDENS the envelope at edge pixels → ringing/overshoot (k3, hyb50), or
    (b) Is converted to a flat cap by a global safety ceiling → softer images
        that are closer to faithful (cap10), defeating the purpose of F3.

==============================================================================
7. PASS/REJECT RECOMMENDATION
==============================================================================

REJECT — all four tested self-envelope variants fail:

  • Variant A (k3, no cap):  FAILS — PSNR −0.3 to −4.6 dB, severe ringing.
  • Variant B (cap10):       FAILS — 25–35% sharpness loss (material
                              capability regression).
  • Variant C (hybrid_50):   FAILS — same ringing as Variant A.
  • Variant D (local-σ):     FAILS — no measurable benefit; computationally
                              a no-op for F3's specific delta distribution.

ADDITIONALLY:
  • No variant creates noise amplification (noise metric unchanged).
  • No variant creates invented structure (edge_align = 1.0 everywhere).
  • Sky/flat regions and roads are unaffected in all variants (edge gate
    already suppresses F3 in those areas — the envelope is irrelevant there).

CONCLUSION:
  F3's flat ±14 envelope is the correct design for its specific delta
  characteristics (cross-image band-pass structure scaled by w=6.0).
  The self-envelope philosophy does not transfer to F3 because F3's
  delta is geometrically different from G7/A+/G9's self-contained
  high-pass residual. The existing production F3 algorithm should not
  be modified in this direction.
